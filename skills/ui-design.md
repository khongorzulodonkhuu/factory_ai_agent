# Skill: UI design (coder and designer agents)
Aim for a polished, modern product that feels like the category leader, not a demo. Use only the design tokens (bg-primary, bg-surface, bg-background, text-text, text-text-muted, border-border, rounded-card, success/warning/danger).

## Header (every signed-in screen), modelled on modern car-lease sites
- Bar: `sticky top-0 z-40 bg-surface shadow-sm`, content in `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-4 sm:px-6 h-[72px] flex items-center gap-6`.
- Left: brand block that sits fully inside the header and never overflows it: `self-stretch -my-px flex items-center bg-primary px-6` (the bar's full height, square edges at top and bottom), product name in white `text-xl font-extrabold tracking-tight`. Nothing in the header may extend below the bar, and page content starts below it with normal spacing.
- Centre: the role's menu right after the brand block, `ml-10 flex items-center gap-7`. Each item is a plain text button `relative py-6 text-[15px] font-medium text-text whitespace-nowrap hover:text-primary` with a small chevron (`<Icon name="chevron" className="h-4 w-4" />`) when it opens a dropdown. The active section is `text-primary` with a 2px bar under it (`after:absolute after:inset-x-0 after:bottom-0 after:h-0.5 after:bg-primary`). No borders, boxes or backgrounds on menu items; the focus ring appears only for keyboard focus (`focus-visible:ring-2 ring-primary/30 rounded-md`). Count badges `ml-1.5 rounded-full bg-primary px-1.5 text-[11px] text-white`.
- Dropdown panels: `absolute mt-3 w-56 rounded-xl border border-border bg-surface p-2 shadow-lg`, items `block w-full rounded-lg px-3 py-2 text-left text-sm hover:bg-background`. They open on click, close on outside click or Escape, are keyboard reachable (`aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`), and every item goes to a real view. No dead links.
- The header must fit on one line at 1280px in both languages: at most 4 top-level menu items (put the rest inside dropdowns), labels `whitespace-nowrap`, short Mongolian labels ("Машин түрээслэх", "Миний аялал", "Тусламж"), and the user's name hidden below xl.
- No Help, FAQ or support items in the header: the FAQ lives at the bottom of the renter home page and is linked from the footer.
- Menus are worded for the person using them. Never use vague words like "Discover", "Open" or "Resources".
  - Renter, someone who wants to rent a car:
    - "Rent a car ▾": All cars, Saved cars (badge), Popular makes
    - "My bookings ▾": Upcoming (badge), Active, Completed (the brief's My Bookings dashboard, with status tags Pending, Confirmed, Completed)
  - Owner, someone renting out cars:
    - "Dashboard": the owner home, with the earnings tracker (completed rentals and total revenue) as cards
    - "My fleet ▾": My cars, + List a car
    - "Requests" (badge = pending)
- Right cluster pushed to the far right with `ml-auto flex items-center gap-5`, in this order:
  1. Owners only: "+ List a car" pill (it opens the new-car form directly, in one click, from any page) `rounded-full bg-success px-5 h-10 text-sm font-semibold text-white`. Renters get no button here: searching happens in the page's search card, so "Find a car" is never repeated in the header.
  2. The compact `<LanguageToggle />`.
  3. A thin divider `<span aria-hidden="true" className="h-6 w-px bg-border" />`.
  4. One account button (test id "account-menu-btn") that groups the user: `flex items-center gap-2.5 rounded-full py-1 pl-1 pr-3 hover:bg-background`, with the initials avatar `h-9 w-9 rounded-full bg-primary/10 text-primary text-sm font-bold grid place-items-center`, then name `text-sm font-semibold` over role `text-xs text-text-muted` (hidden below xl), then a chevron. It opens the account menu: name, role pill, a divider and "Log out" (test id "logout-btn"). Never put "Log out" or a separate account icon directly in the header bar.
- The page title area under the header greets by role on the main view (e.g. "Find your next car" for renters, "Your fleet today" for owners).
- Below lg: the centre menu collapses into a menu button (`mobile-menu-btn`) opening a full-width panel with the same groups; the account button, main action and language toggle stay visible.
- The login screen shows only the brand block at the left and `<LanguageToggle />` at the right.

## Footer (every signed-in screen, and the login screen)
- `footer` with test id "site-footer": `mt-16 border-t border-border bg-surface`, content in `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-4 sm:px-6 py-10`.
- Top part, `grid gap-8 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`:
  1. Brand column: the product name in `text-lg font-extrabold text-primary` and a one-line tagline in `text-sm text-text-muted`.
  2. "For renters": links to Browse cars, My bookings, FAQ.
  3. "For owners": links to List a car, Booking requests, Dashboard.
  4. "Wheels2Go": How pricing works (opens the FAQ), Pickup locations (scrolls to the search card), and `<LanguageToggle />`.
  Column headings are `text-sm font-semibold text-text`; links are `text-sm text-text-muted hover:text-primary` buttons that switch views (no router, no dead links). A link to another role's page is shown only to that role, or it opens the login screen.
- Bottom bar: `mt-8 border-t border-border pt-6 flex flex-col sm:flex-row justify-between gap-2 text-xs text-text-muted` with "© {year} Wheels2Go" (year from the current date) on the left and "Prices in USD · shown in ₮ in Mongolian" on the right.
- Never invent contact details, addresses, phone numbers, social links or legal pages. Every footer item must work or be plain text. All footer text goes through t().

## Layout and components
- Page: `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-4 sm:px-6 py-6 sm:py-8`; page title text-2xl font-semibold with a one-line muted subtitle; primary page action aligned right of the title.
- Cards: `rounded-card border border-border bg-surface shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`; images `aspect-[16/10] w-full object-cover` on top with the status/location badge overlaid in a corner.
- Grids: 1 column on mobile, 2 at sm, 3 at lg (4 at xl for dense catalogs); gap-4 to gap-6.
- Buttons: one primary per area (bg-primary text-white), secondary is bordered, destructive uses danger; all have hover, disabled and `focus-visible:ring-2 ring-primary/40` states; min height 40px.
- Forms: labels above inputs, helper/error text below, two columns on md+, grouped in sections with headings; required fields marked.
- Required fields = only what the brief names for that form (for car listings: make, model, year, daily rate, cleaning fee, location). Every extra attribute is optional with a sensible default pre-filled (seats 5, bags 2, odometer 0 km, mileage Unlimited, body type and fuel/transmission preselected to the most common value). Location is one required free-text field ("Airport Hub" or a full address is enough to save); city, area and country are optional extras. Never ask users for coordinates: maps work from the address text.
- Date + time pickers come in pairs: a native date input and, next to it, a time select (`select` with 30-minute steps from 06:00 to 22:00, default 10:00, same chevron wrapper as other selects), under one label ("Pickup" / "Return") in a `grid grid-cols-[1fr_7rem] gap-2` row. The home search card, the car details booking box and checkout all use the same pair.
- Form controls share one look: label `mb-1.5 block text-sm font-medium text-text`; inputs and selects `h-11 w-full rounded-lg border border-border bg-surface px-3.5 text-sm text-text placeholder:text-text-muted focus:border-primary focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary/20`; fields stacked with `space-y-4` (or `gap-4` in grids).
- Never use the browser's default select arrow (it sticks to the border). Every <select> is wrapped so the arrow has room:
  `<div className="relative"><select className="... appearance-none pr-10 cursor-pointer">...</select><Icon name="chevron" className="pointer-events-none absolute right-3 top-1/2 h-4 w-4 -translate-y-1/2 text-text-muted" /></div>`
  Same for search inputs: the search icon sits at `left-3` and the input gets `pl-10`. Number inputs that show a unit put it in a right-side suffix (`pr-14`, unit at `right-3`).
- Filters: live in the home search card (see Renter home below); "Clear filters" sits in that card's corner.
- Status badges: always the scaffold's `<StatusBadge />` (solid white pill, bold coloured text and dot, shadow). On a photo place it at `absolute left-3 top-3`. Never put status text on a translucent or tinted background.
- The design tokens must make these readable: success a clear green (e.g. #15803d), warning an orange (e.g. #c2410c), both with at least 4.5:1 contrast on white.
- Show each piece of information once per screen (one price summary, one booking form); never duplicate a widget in two panels of the same view.
- Use the exact action words from the brief and its tests on buttons (booking requests always use "Approve" / "Decline", never "Confirm" or "Accept"), not synonyms.
- Money and key numbers: tabular-nums; totals larger and bold.
- Empty states: icon-like emoji or initial, one sentence, and the next action button. Never show a blank area. A renter with no trips sees "No bookings yet" and a "Find a car" button.
- Booking and request lists are sorted newest first and grouped by status tabs (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, with counts). Items created in this session carry a small "New" badge (`rounded-full bg-accent/15 text-accent px-2 text-xs font-semibold`) so they never get lost among older ones; each row shows the renter's name, the car, the dates and the total.
- Long Mongolian text must wrap, never overflow buttons or tabs (use whitespace-normal and min-w-0 on flex children).

## Marketplace patterns (as on leading car-rental sites; adopt the structure, never their brand or claims)
- Renter home (Browse), top to bottom, modelled on modern car-lease sites:
  1. Photo hero: full-width `relative h-[340px] sm:h-[420px] overflow-hidden` with the image of the highest-rated car (`absolute inset-0 h-full w-full object-cover`, onError -> `bg-gradient-to-br from-primary to-primary-dark`), two overlays for legible white text on any photo (`absolute inset-0 bg-black/35` plus `bg-gradient-to-t from-black/70 via-black/30 to-transparent`), headline and subtitle with `drop-shadow-lg`, and the headline block centred horizontally in the hero, above the search card: `absolute inset-x-0 bottom-28 sm:bottom-32 mx-auto max-w-3xl px-4 text-center text-white`; headline `text-3xl sm:text-5xl font-extrabold leading-tight [text-wrap:balance]` kept to two balanced lines (the max-w-3xl width makes long headlines like "Rent a car from local owners" break into two lines, in both languages); subtitle under it `mt-3 mx-auto max-w-2xl text-base sm:text-lg text-white/85 [text-wrap:balance]`, also centred.
  2. Search card overlapping the hero: `relative z-10 -mt-24 mx-auto max-w-6xl rounded-2xl bg-surface p-5 sm:p-6 shadow-xl`. Row 1: label "Find your car" (`text-base font-semibold`) then dropdowns in `rounded-lg border border-border h-11 px-3 text-sm` for fuel type, body type, transmission and make, then a max-daily-rate control: the label, then the range slider on its own full-width row (`w-full min-w-[10rem] accent-primary`, never squeezed into a narrow grid cell where only the thumb is visible), and under it the number input with the USD suffix bound to the same value, plus the current amount via useMoney. Row 2: a wide text search with a search icon (make or model, location keyword) and a large CTA `h-12 rounded-lg bg-primary px-6 text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-wide text-white` reading "Show results ({count} cars)" with the live result count; clicking it scrolls to the results. This card IS the filter panel: filters apply live, there is no second filter sidebar, and "Clear filters" sits in its corner.
  3. Results, top to bottom: (a) the make strip directly above the results toolbar, as the results' own make filter: an "All" item followed by every make in the data with its count as a small superscript (e.g. Ford¹), large wordmarks `text-2xl font-bold text-text-muted/60 hover:text-text` evenly spaced in one row (wrapping on mobile), the selected make `text-primary` and "All" selected when no make is chosen; clicking a make filters the results below and syncs with the search card's make dropdown, text only, never third-party logos; (b) the toolbar with "{count} cars available" (the total across all pages), sort select (Recommended, Price: low to high, Price: high to low) and grid/list toggle, then the catalog grid of "car-card" cards; dates picked on a car's page carry into its checkout. "Recommended" lists the newest listings first, so a car an owner just added is always on page 1.
  Pagination under the grid: 6 cards per page; `nav aria-label="Pagination"` (test id "pagination") centred, with "Previous" / "Next" buttons (test ids "page-prev", "page-next", disabled at the ends) and numbered page buttons (test id "page-btn", the current one `bg-primary text-white` with aria-current="page", others bordered `h-9 min-w-9 rounded-lg text-sm`), plus "Showing {from}-{to} of {total}" in `text-sm text-text-muted`. Changing any filter, search or sort returns to page 1; changing page scrolls to the top of the results. Hide the pagination when everything fits on one page.
  4. A row of 3 short reassurance points backed by real features only (e.g. "Full price breakdown before you book", "Exact pickup address and instructions", "Owners confirm every request"). Never claim guarantees, insurance or cancellation terms the app does not implement.
  5. "Top picks this week 🔥", placed after the results and directly above the FAQ (never between the search card and the results): centered `text-3xl font-bold` heading and the 3 highest-rated cars as cards with test id "featured-car-card" (not "car-card", so catalog counts stay exact).
  6. The FAQ, last on the page.
- Car card (grid, the default view; modelled on modern lease/rental listings, compact type scale):
  - Container: `group overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-border bg-surface shadow-sm transition hover:shadow-md`, the whole card is clickable (opens details) and keyboard focusable; grid `sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-6`.
  - Image: `aspect-[16/10] w-full object-cover` (onError placeholder) with one corner badge at top-right `absolute right-3 top-3 rounded-md px-3 py-1 text-sm font-semibold text-white shadow` backed by data only: "Top rated" (owner rating >= 9, bg-success), "New listing" (added this session or newest in data, bg-danger), "Electric" (bg-primary). No badge when none applies.
  - Body `p-4`: row 1 = title `text-base (16px) font-semibold uppercase tracking-wide text-text truncate` (make + model) and the year on the right `text-sm text-text-muted`; row 2 = description `text-sm (14px) text-text-muted` "Body type · Seats · Odometer" (e.g. "SUV · 5 seats · 45,000 km"); row 3 = owner line `text-sm` with star icon, rating and owner name, or "New owner".
  - Chips `mt-3 flex flex-wrap gap-2`, each `rounded-full px-3 py-1 text-sm`: fuel chip highlighted `bg-primary/10 text-primary font-medium`, others `bg-background text-text` (transmission, bags, mileage policy, location with the location icon).
  - Footer `mt-4 flex items-end justify-between`: price `text-lg (18px) font-bold tabular-nums whitespace-nowrap` via useMoney, then `text-sm text-text-muted` "per day" (the card never shows the cleaning fee: it appears only on the car details/checkout price breakdown); right side = heart icon button (save to favourites, persisted in localStorage, `aria-pressed`, filled danger colour when saved) and a small `text-sm font-semibold text-primary` "View deal" link.
  - Type scale is fixed for cards: 16px titles, 14px everything else, 18px bold price. Never larger inside a card.
  - List view (optional toggle) shows the same content in one row: image left (w-56), text middle, price + actions right, same type scale.
  - Only show facts that exist in the data; never show sponsored/"Ad" labels, cancellation promises or features the app does not have.
- Car details/checkout: a spec table with every attribute including odometer ("45,000 km") and mileage policy, a 3-step indicator (Choose car → Dates & price → Request sent), a "What's included" checklist built from the listing data (pickup instructions, cleaning fee, seats, transmission), and the price breakdown in a sticky right column with the primary action under the total.
- Car details: the "Pickup & return" card ends with the car owner, below a `border-t border-border pt-4 mt-4` divider (test id "car-owner"): initials avatar `h-11 w-11 rounded-full bg-primary/10 text-primary font-bold grid place-items-center`, the label "Car owner" (`text-xs text-text-muted`), the owner's name (`text-base font-semibold`), and beside it the owner rating with a star icon and the number of cars they list, all from the data (never phone numbers, emails, reviews or "verified" badges the app does not have). Look the owner up from the users by the car's owner id; hide the block if no owner is found.
- The FAQ has 3-4 questions about price calculation, cleaning fee, pickup and approval, using <details>/<summary> accordions.
